# Reflection

The combined questions most tested the boundary between correct worker results and reliable synthesis. For example, the deployed system correctly combined four active projects with the $500 annual equipment allowance, yet an additional FAQ claim pointed to the wrong excerpt. The retail answer also reported the requested 34% waste and 19% stockout reductions correctly while attaching background details to an excerpt that did not contain them. These were synthesis and citation-attribution errors, not unsafe-SQL or routing failures. I addressed them by limiting synthesis to the requested worker claims, preserving their source references, and checking the actual excerpts rather than relying on citation-format checks alone. A separate review caught the interpretation of one client row as one contract, which the schema cannot establish. This showed me that SQL validation protects execution, while reliable multi-agent answers also require clear data semantics, careful synthesis and factual evaluation. Memory and exact-query confirmation passed together locally and in Lambda; the controlled replay measurements also showed that reliability and conversational context have a measurable input-token cost.
